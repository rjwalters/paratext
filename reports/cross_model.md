## How to read this report

- Each cell is the **mean delta vs `polished_neutral`** for that model on that variant class, followed by a 95% percentile bootstrap CI in brackets. A CI excluding 0 is the rough significance bar.
- **Sensitivity:** how big is the fatigue_coded delta? Larger = the model reacts more strongly to fatigue cues.
- **Specificity:** is the `fatigue_coded` delta larger than the `random_typo_control` delta? If they look the same, the model is responding to noise rather than cues. The decoupling experiment on Opus 4.7 suggested both contribute independently.
- **Calibration:** look at the `cue_only` row — that's contextual fatigue cues with NO typos. A well-calibrated model should still register some fatigue (the cues are real) but less than full fatigue_coded.
